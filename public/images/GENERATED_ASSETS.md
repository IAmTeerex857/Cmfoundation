# Image asset notes

## Generated editorial imagery

The files in `generated/` are AI-generated, photorealistic editorial illustrations created for this website. They replace generic Unsplash stock in the current build, but they are **not documentary photographs of CMF activities**.

In particular, these milestone images are illustrative and should be replaced when verified event photography is supplied:

- `generated/childrens-ministry.jpg`
- `generated/award-ceremony.jpg`
- `generated/literature-launch.jpg`

The remaining generated scenes cover education, community projects, book distribution, family support, volunteering, partnerships, reading and the literature archive. They contain no CMF logo or claim to depict a named real event.

## Generated cover artwork

The eleven original JPG files directly in `covers/` are AI-generated cover backgrounds. Six remain active interim covers with live HTML title/author overlays. Five have been superseded by supplied complete covers under `covers/official/`; the superseded generated files remain unused. Official covers display without text overlays, shading, or cropping. See `SUPPLIED_ASSETS.md` for source files and mappings. Replace the remaining six generated covers when supplied.

## Generation prompt set

All generated assets used the built-in image-generation tool.

Editorial-photo base prompt: photorealistic candid documentary/editorial photography in an authentic contemporary Nigerian setting; natural skin, fabric and architectural texture; true-to-life colour; composition safe for the site's responsive crop; no logos, readable text or watermark; avoid staged charity-ad aesthetics, distorted hands and duplicated people.

Scene variations: student reading aloud; community tree planting; dignified family support; school book distribution; teenager reading; literature archive; education-support portrait; volunteer literacy gathering; nonprofit partnership meeting; family reading; children's ministry reading session; illustrative posthumous-award handover; illustrative Abuja literature launch.

Cover-art base prompt: premium realistic full-bleed editorial artwork in a 2:3 portrait ratio; symbolic subject in the lower-middle; calm negative space at top and bottom for live title/author typography; tactile realistic materials; strong thumbnail silhouette; no text, letters, logos, border, book mockup or watermark.

Cover variations: divided paths at sunrise; servant leader following a community; cross at blue dawn; three interlocking gold rings; cross over cracked earth at sunset; open prison door facing three crosses; leafless tree and hazy city reflection; scales and case file on a legal desk; unused gavel with balanced scales; antique trumpet beneath an opening sky; open gate and winding highland path.
