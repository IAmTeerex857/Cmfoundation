# Guide for CMF staff

For the Foundation's team in Abuja who look after the website day to day.

## Signing in

1. Go to **cm.foundation/admin**.
2. Enter your authorised CMF email address and the management password, then press **Sign in**.

Only addresses on the authorised list (`ADMIN_EMAILS`) can open the dashboard, and only with the password (`ADMIN_PASSWORD`),
both kept in the hosting settings, never in the code. To add someone or change the password, ask the web developer.
You stay signed in on that device for 400 days (the longest browsers allow); use **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar on shared computers.

## Every morning

1. Read the **daily management summary** email.
2. Open **Inbox** and work through anything marked **new**:
   - **Reader feedback**: reply by email if the reader left an address, then set the status to *replied* or *read*.
   - **Order enquiries**: reply with pricing, delivery arrangements and availability; set *in-progress*, then *done*.
   - **Enquiries** (partnership, sponsorship, in-kind help, volunteering, events, media): reply or pass to the right person.
3. Check the **Overview** for anything unusual.

## Recording donations

Record a gift **only after you have confirmed it** in the payment partner's dashboard or on the bank statement.
A visit to the thank-you page is not proof of payment.

1. **Donations & sponsorships → Record donation**.
2. Enter the date received, amount, currency, purpose (the payment link it came through tells you), method and the payment reference.
3. Donor name and email are optional. Use them only for acknowledgements and receipts.

Book orders are **not** donations: they stay in the Inbox. Record sponsorships separately in the same page.

## The dashboard figures

- **Reading starts**: a book opened in the reader. **Completed**: the reader reached the end of the final chapter. A click alone never counts as reading.
- **Completion estimate**: completions ÷ starts, a rough guide.
- **Registrations**: accounts with a confirmed email. **Returning readers**: signed in during the period.
- **Website traffic** (visitors, where they come from, phones or computers, search terms) is in the Looker Studio section or Google Analytics.
- Choose the period at the top right: Today, 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or a year.

## Privacy rules for staff

- Readers' **personal notes are private** and are not available in the dashboard. Never ask a reader for them.
- Use the **newsletter list** (Readers & exports) only for the Foundation's own updates, and only for people who opted in. Remove anyone who unsubscribes.
- Do not copy reader details into personal phones, chats or spreadsheets outside the Foundation's accounts.
- If a reader asks to see or delete their data, they can do it themselves at **cm.foundation/reader**. If they write instead, reply within 30 days.
- If you suspect a data breach (a lost laptop with exports, a strange sign-in email), tell the Trustee and the web developer **the same day**.

## Adding a new book

Send the developer the final EPUB, front and back covers (no spine), and the bibliographic details (subtitle, year, edition, ISBN, pages).
The book gets its own page and becomes readable once it is added. Until then it shows **Coming Soon** or **Not Yet Online**.
